- Fixed: an orbit now actually drives the gimbal and pulls focus in the exported file. The per-waypoint gimbal angles alone were never enough — flight-verified on a Matrice 4T, the aircraft turned to the target correctly but held one gimbal angle for the whole flight and never focused. Orbits now carry the mechanism that did work: the opening angle set on the first waypoint, the gimbal walked toward the next waypoint's angle on every leg, and a focus once the aircraft has turned to the target.
- Fixed: `gimbalEvenlyRotate` is exported in its own `betweenAdjacentPoints` action group spanning each leg, instead of sitting in the waypoint's `reachPoint` group where it never ran.
