- Added: the **"na červený kruh"** shortcut can be locked (padlock next to it). While locked, the camera target becomes draggable on the map and the whole route travels with it, staying on the ring where the building fits — instead of the flight staying put while the target wanders off the middle of it. Anything else that moves the ring (object height, the building outline, the camera) re-snaps the route too.
- Improved: the oval now deforms as one smooth curve when the mid-arc handle is dragged. It used to hold a fixed distance across the middle and flare only near the ends, which slid a stiff arc around rather than reshaping the path. Every bearing moves, the two ends stay pinned, and there is no limit on how far in the middle can be pulled.
- Fixed: the red "whole building fits" ring now shrinks as the flight altitude rises. From higher up the building subtends a smaller angle — its height is seen more steeply and its length from farther along the slant — so a closer pass is genuinely allowed; the ring used to ignore altitude entirely and stayed put at 50 m or at 100 m.
