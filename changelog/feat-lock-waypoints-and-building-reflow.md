- Added: waypoints can be locked in place — a padlock in each sidebar row, or "Zamknout" in the bulk toolbar for a whole selection. A locked waypoint can't be dragged on the map, sits out whole-mission moves and rotations, survives re-applying the template that created it, and stays put when a building is reshaped. Built for long-running time-lapse contracts: footage from earlier visits only lines up if the points already filmed stay exactly where they were.
- Added: reshaping a building now offers to bring the route with it. Change a footprint or height and a bar appears with the old and new recommended standoff and how many waypoints would move; the map shows dashed leaders to where each one would land. Nothing moves until you click Použít, and locked waypoints never move at all — so a mission can grow with a building under construction without disturbing the shots already in the cut. Each moved waypoint keeps its own bearing around the building and its own margin beyond the recommended radius, so the route's shape and order survive; altitude, speed and gimbal pitch are untouched.
