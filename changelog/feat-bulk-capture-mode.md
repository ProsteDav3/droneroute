- Added: photo or video for any selection of waypoints. The bulk-edit panel ("Upravit" with two or more waypoints selected) now has the same "Záznam" choice as the templates — Foto, Video and Cinema video. A mission imported from a KMZ arrives without its template settings, so until now the only way to turn a video orbit into a photo one was to rebuild every waypoint's actions by hand. Select all, open "Upravit", pick the mode. Only photo and recording actions are rewritten; the gimbal moves, hover and focus already on the waypoints stay in place and still run before the shot. The panel lights up the mode the selection already uses, so an imported video mission shows "Video" straight away.
- Added: a warning when that leaves the mission's recording unpaired. Changing only part of a route can leave a second start while the camera is already rolling, or a stop with nothing to stop — the aircraft accepts such a file and quietly records something else. The editor now names the waypoint so the other end can be fixed before export.
- Fixed: on a phone the bulk toolbar was hidden under the sidebar drawer. Waypoints are selected in that drawer's list, so every bulk action — including the new photo/video choice — sat underneath it and could not be tapped. The toolbar now sits above the drawer. It also spans the phone's width and wraps onto a second line instead of scrolling sideways, so "Upravit" is on screen without hunting for it. Before, the bar only ever had half the screen to fit into.
