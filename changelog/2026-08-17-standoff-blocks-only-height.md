## Summary

Stop a long building's own length blocking a close orbit — and freezing the centre handle. The hard guard now only enforces what the subject's height needs; fitting the building end-to-end became advice.

## Changes

- The standoff guard added yesterday required enough room for the whole building to fit **end-to-end**. For a 70 m hall that is ~57 m where its 10 m height needs ~10.5 m, so a perfectly reasonable orbit at 28 m was blocked from being applied. Worse, because the requirement then exceeded the radius, the centre-drag clamp resolved to a single point: the handle could not be moved at all, inside the guide ring or out. Same story on a 142 m building at a 110 m radius.
- The guard (and the centre-drag clamp) now use the object's **height** requirement only — the case it was actually asked for, where a waypoint ends up practically overhead and the subject doesn't fit even vertically. Its message says as much.
- The building-length requirement is now a plain advisory line: "Nejbližší bod je 28 m od budovy — v záběru bude jen její část, na celou délku by bylo potřeba 57 m. U dlouhé budovy je let zblízka běžná volba; použít to jde." It never disables Apply.
