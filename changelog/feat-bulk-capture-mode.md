- Added: photo or video for any selection of waypoints. The bulk-edit panel ("Upravit" with two or more waypoints selected) now has the same "Záznam" choice as the templates — Foto, Video and Cinema video. A mission imported from a KMZ arrives without its template settings, so until now the only way to turn a video orbit into a photo one was to rebuild every waypoint's actions by hand. Select all, open "Upravit", pick the mode. Only photo and recording actions are rewritten; the gimbal moves, hover and focus already on the waypoints stay in place and still run before the shot. The panel lights up the mode the selection already uses, so an imported video mission shows "Video" straight away.
