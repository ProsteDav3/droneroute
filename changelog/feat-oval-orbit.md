- Added: an orbit around a locked target can now be an **oval**. Drag the violet handle at the middle of the arc toward the centre (or type the distance into "Střed oblouku") and the far side of the flight pulls in, while the start and end of the arc stay exactly where you placed them. With the target off to one side the middle of the arc is the point farthest from it — on the Congress Centre orbit 147 m against 74 m at the ends, so the building shrank to half over the shot; pulling the middle to 60 m brings that to 109 m and the size change from 2.0× down to 1.5×. Gimbal angles, the framing warnings and the clearance guard all measure the oval that will actually be flown.
