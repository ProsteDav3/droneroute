- Fixed: deleting a saved route works again. Five tables carry a foreign key to the mission and none declared `ON DELETE CASCADE`, while saving a mission writes a version snapshot straight away — so every delete failed with a foreign-key error and the route stayed in the library. That is why libraries filled up with hundreds of uncleanable segment routes. The mission's own history (comments, snapshots, risk assessment, permits) now goes with it; flight logs and live-track sessions are records of something that actually happened and are unlinked instead of deleted.
- Added: routes can be ticked and deleted in bulk — a checkbox on each card, "Vybrat vše", and "Smazat vybrané (N)". One request for the whole batch, and only rows you own are touched.
- Fixed: clearing DJI Cloud waylines in bulk no longer stops after ten files. The per-file deletes ran into our own strict rate limiter (10 per minute) and reported "smazáno 10 z 50"; the whole batch now goes in a single request.
