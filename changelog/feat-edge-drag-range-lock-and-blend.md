- Added: drag a whole wall of a building. The handle in the middle of each edge is now draggable and takes both its corners with it, keeping the wall parallel — previously reshaping one side meant dragging its two corners separately and confirming the reflow twice. Clicking that handle still inserts a corner, as before.
- Added: lock a range of waypoints by typing it. The row above the waypoint list takes a from/to pair ("Body 1 – 40") and locks or unlocks exactly those. On a 72-point orbit, finding both ends of the range in a scrolling list was the slowest part of the job.
- Added: the reflow now eases into a locked stretch instead of stepping. The first waypoint after the locked run barely moves and the move grows to full size over the next few, so the flight path no longer takes a visible kink exactly where the already-filmed half ends — which is where two visits get cut together. The "Přechod" field in the reflow bar sets the ramp width (8 waypoints by default, 0 for the old hard step) and the map preview follows it live.
