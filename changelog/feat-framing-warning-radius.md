- Improved: the orbit panel's framing warnings now also state the **radius** that would satisfy them, not only the required distance from the subject. With a locked POI off the orbit's centre those are different numbers (a 100 m radius can put the near side of the arc 67 m from the building), so the required distance alone was not something you could type into the radius field.
- Fixed: the radius field's "fits from / ideal" hint is measured on the nearest distance actually flown when the POI is locked off-centre, and says so — it used to compare those distances against the radius, which could show a tick while the near end of the arc was already cropping the building.
